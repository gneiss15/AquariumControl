The PolyForm Noncommercial license at the repository root applies to the author's own AquariumControl files. Existing third-party licenses, author notices and hardware manufacturers' rights remain in effect.

- `Libs/GnBasicLib/LICENSE` and `Libs/GnWifiKit8Lib/LICENSE`: MIT license; retain their terms and notices.
- Other libraries and source files retain the author and license notices included in their files. Libraries installed by the ESP/Arduino toolchains are not bundled here.
- Purchased modules, manufacturer CAD geometry and reference illustrations are not relicensed by AquariumControl. The included WiFi Kit 8 illustration is derived from the pinout reference supplied with the project. Manufacturer names identify compatible hardware.
- Original manufacturer PDF manuals are not included in the public export. Refer to the suppliers' documentation for pin assignments and relay-module specifications.

Hardware descriptions are work in progress. The existing installation has been used, but variants and incomplete build guides are identified in their respective READMEs.
